Professional learning

Workshops

Professional learning for schools, regional PD consortia and the ATA Science Council conference.

Like the meeting of two rivers, true foundational learning happens at the intersection of different ways of knowing. Our workshops help Alberta educators attend to TQS5 beyond compliance. We bring the curriculum resources you need for tomorrow's lesson, and we build the psychological safety required to transform your pedagogy for the future.

Truth and Reconciliation

Attending to TQS5 beyond compliance

Move past checklists toward meaningful practice. We look at how teachers come to understand and interpret TQS5, and how that understanding grows into confident integration in the classroom.

Pedagogy

Building psychological safety to transform pedagogy

Real change in teaching needs room to try, question and learn. This session focuses on the conditions that let teachers and teams take that step together.
