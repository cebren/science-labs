Our Commitment & Positionality

As a European Settler, Dr. Pedersen deeply acknowledges his positionality in this space. His doctoral research focused primarily on non-Indigenous teachers and their unique understandings, interpretations, and journeys of integrating TQS5.

We are fully committed to walking this path alongside and in partnership with Indigenous Elders, Knowledge Keepers, and communities.

If you are doing this work, please reach out. We would love to connect and collaborate.

Dr. Lance Pedersen

Dr. Pedersen is the Science department head at a Grades 7 to 9 school in Edmonton. He earned his EdD from the University of Portland in 2026. His dissertation, Reconciliation Through Education: The Journey Beyond Compliance, was nominated for a Dissertation in Practice of the Year Award. He also builds interactive web tools for classroom use, including the Alberta Science (Grades 7–9) Starter Pack.
