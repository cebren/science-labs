Using the Starter Pack

Free for classroom use

Teachers may use the labs and slide decks with their classes at no cost. Please credit Dr. Lance Pedersen when you share them.

Student privacy

Students do not sign in. A lab saves progress only in the student's own browser on that device. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

Slide decks

Each lab has a companion slide deck. Each deck opens in its own viewer, ready to present to your class. Use the arrow keys to move between slides, and Teacher notes to see the speaker notes.

Download and edit the slides

Every deck has a Download for PowerPoint or Google Slides button. The file is fully editable, and the teacher notes come along as speaker notes.

To open it in Google Slides: download the file, then go to Google Drive. Choose New, then File upload, and pick the file. Double-click it in Drive and choose Open with Google Slides. To keep editing in Google Slides, choose File, then Save as Google Slides.

In PowerPoint or Keynote: open the downloaded file. The decks use three free Google fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Atkinson Hyperlegible and JetBrains Mono. Google Slides has them built in. For PowerPoint, install them from fonts.google.com. Otherwise PowerPoint substitutes similar fonts and some lines may wrap differently.

Decks are being added to the site over the next day. Until a deck is added, its button opens the deck in its original viewer.
