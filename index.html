Moving Truth and Reconciliation Beyond Compliance.

Led by Dr. Pedersen, Confluence Curriculum & PD partners with Alberta educators to attend to TQS5 beyond compliance. We help develop the curriculum resources you need for tomorrow's lesson, and the psychological safety required to transform your pedagogy for the future.
